Mirror Motion guides users through their dance practice journey starting with authentication, where they can log in or create a new account. Once authenticated, users upload a dance video and classify it as a reference video they want to practice with. Next, they can upload practice videos and associate each one with a specific reference video from their library. The Video Library displays all reference videos, where users can click into any reference video to view its associated practice attempts. For each practice video, users can click "Generate Feedback" to initiate an analysis process where the app compares the audio from both videos to find synchronized sections, extracts pose data from each frame, and calculates accuracy scores along with specific timestamps for improvement. Additionally, users can click "Play Synchronized" to watch both videos simultaneously with pose data overlays, enabling direct visual comparison of movements. This comprehensive feedback system helps users track their progress and identify specific areas for improvement in their dance practice.
